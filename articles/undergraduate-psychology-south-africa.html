What Is Undergraduate Psychology in South Africa?

An overview of undergraduate psychology study, what students may study and what further education may be required for professional psychology careers.

What Is Undergraduate Psychology?

Undergraduate psychology refers to the study of Psychology at an undergraduate level. It introduces students to the scientific study of human behaviour, mental processes and the factors that influence how people think, feel and behave.

Psychology may be offered in different types of undergraduate qualifications, depending on the institution and programme.

Students may encounter Psychology as a major, specialisation, subject or through individual Psychology modules within their qualification.

The structure of undergraduate Psychology therefore differs between institutions and qualifications.

Students should review the official programme information of the institution they are considering to understand how Psychology is offered.

What Do Students Study?

The content of an undergraduate Psychology programme varies between institutions and qualifications. Students may encounter areas such as social psychology, developmental psychology, cognitive psychology, research methods and other areas concerned with human behaviour and psychological processes.

Undergraduate Psychology may also involve research and academic skills. Students can develop skills in critical thinking, communication, research, analysing information and applying psychological concepts to different contexts.

The specific subjects, modules and structure of a programme depend on the institution and qualification.

Prospective students should consult the official programme information of the institution they wish to attend.

Which Qualifications Can Include Psychology?

Psychology can form part of different undergraduate qualifications in South Africa. The exact qualification and structure depend on the institution.

Psychology may be offered as a major, specialisation, subject or through individual Psychology modules within a qualification. The way Psychology is incorporated into a programme can therefore differ between institutions.

Students should not assume that Psychology is offered through the same qualification structure at every institution.

When considering a programme, students should check the institution's official website to determine how Psychology is offered and which requirements apply to the qualification.

How Long Does Undergraduate Psychology Take?

The length of undergraduate study depends on the qualification and institution. Some undergraduate qualifications may take around three years to complete, while others may take longer depending on the programme structure and requirements.

Students should check the official programme information of the institution they are considering for the expected duration, admission requirements and curriculum structure.

Can You Become a Psychologist With Undergraduate Psychology?

Completing undergraduate Psychology does not by itself qualify a person to practise as a registered psychologist in South Africa.

Students who intend to pursue a professional career as a psychologist generally need to continue with the relevant postgraduate education, professional training and registration requirements applicable to the professional psychology category they wish to pursue.

The requirements can differ according to the professional pathway. Students should research the requirements for their intended career and consult the relevant official organisations before making decisions about their education and training.

What Can You Do With Undergraduate Psychology?

Undergraduate Psychology can provide students with knowledge and transferable skills that may be relevant to different areas of further study and employment.

Students may develop skills in areas such as communication, critical thinking, research, analysing information and understanding human behaviour.

An undergraduate qualification should not, however, be presented as equivalent to professional registration as a psychologist. Students interested in a specific psychology career should research the education, training and registration requirements for that career.

Where Can You Study Undergraduate Psychology?

Undergraduate Psychology is offered by higher education institutions across South Africa.

The availability of Psychology and the way it is structured within a qualification can differ between institutions.

Students can research institutions that offer undergraduate Psychology and compare the qualifications, programme structures and admission requirements available to them.

Students should consult the official website of the institution they are considering to confirm whether Psychology is available, how it is incorporated into the qualification and what requirements apply.

Where Can You Find Undergraduate Psychology Funding?

Students considering undergraduate Psychology can explore bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities that may support their studies.

Eligibility requirements, application dates and funding conditions differ between funding providers. Students should review the requirements of each funding opportunity and confirm the latest information directly with the relevant funding provider.

Important Information

Undergraduate Psychology programmes, admission requirements, qualification structures and progression pathways may differ between institutions. Information may also change over time.

Always consult the official website of the relevant institution and the appropriate professional body for the most current information before applying or making decisions about your studies.
