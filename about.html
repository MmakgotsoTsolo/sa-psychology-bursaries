HELPING PSYCHOLOGY STUDENTS ACROSS SOUTH AFRICA

Who We Are

SA Psychology Bursaries is an independent website created to help undergraduate and postgraduate psychology students in South Africa discover bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make psychology funding information easier to access by bringing together bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities in one place.

Why We Created This Website

Searching for psychology funding can be stressful, confusing and time-consuming. We created this website to simplify the process by providing a central place where students can explore funding opportunities and access official application information.

Our Vision

We aim to become South Africa's trusted source of psychology funding information, helping more students access opportunities that support their academic and professional goals.
