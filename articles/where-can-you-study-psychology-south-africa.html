Where Can You Study Psychology in South Africa?

Information on how to find undergraduate and postgraduate psychology programmes in South Africa and what to consider when choosing where to study.

Where Can You Study Psychology in South Africa?

Psychology can be studied at a number of higher education institutions across South Africa. The qualifications and psychology programmes available can differ between institutions.

Students may find psychology as part of undergraduate qualifications, while postgraduate students may find Honours, Master's and Doctoral programmes depending on the institution and the areas of psychology it offers.

When deciding where to study, students should look beyond the name of the institution and carefully review the specific psychology qualification and programme information available.

Where Can You Study Undergraduate Psychology?

Undergraduate psychology is available through different types of bachelor's degrees and other undergraduate qualifications, depending on the institution.

Psychology may be offered as a major, subject or part of a broader qualification. The structure can therefore differ between institutions.

Students interested in undergraduate psychology should check the official website of each institution to determine whether psychology is available, which qualification includes it and what the admission requirements are.

Where Can You Study Postgraduate Psychology?

Postgraduate psychology programmes are offered by institutions that provide the relevant postgraduate qualifications. These may include Honours, Master's and Doctoral programmes.

The postgraduate psychology programmes available can differ between institutions. Some may offer particular areas of psychology or specific types of postgraduate study that are not available elsewhere.

Students should review the official postgraduate programme information to determine which qualifications are available, whether applications are open and what academic requirements apply.

Public Higher Education Institutions

Public higher education institutions in South Africa include universities and other institutions that provide accredited higher education programmes.

Students interested in psychology can explore the official websites of public institutions to identify undergraduate and postgraduate psychology programmes that may be available.

The availability of psychology programmes, admission requirements and programme structures can vary between institutions. Students should therefore check the official information for the specific programme they are considering.

Private Higher Education Institutions

Private higher education institutions may also offer qualifications that include psychology or related fields of study.

Students considering a private institution should confirm that the qualification is appropriately registered and accredited before applying.

Students should also review the official programme information to understand the qualification structure, admission requirements, fees and progression opportunities.

How Can You Find a Psychology Programme?

A useful starting point is to visit the official website of the higher education institution and search for its psychology qualifications or programmes.

Students can look for information under areas such as undergraduate studies, postgraduate studies, faculties, schools, departments or programme directories.

It is important to search for the specific qualification rather than relying only on the word "Psychology". Different programmes may include psychology in different ways.

What Should You Consider When Choosing Where to Study?

Students should consider several factors when comparing psychology programmes. These may include admission requirements, qualification structure, duration, tuition fees, location, study options and the areas of psychology covered by the programme.

Students who intend to pursue a professional psychology career should also consider whether the programme forms part of the relevant educational pathway and what further education, training and registration requirements may apply.

The most suitable programme will depend on the student's academic circumstances, career plans and the requirements of the qualification.

Check the Official Programme Information

Programme information can change from one academic year to another. Admission requirements, available modules, application dates, fees and programme structures may therefore differ over time.

Before applying, students should always confirm the latest information directly through the official website of the institution offering the programme.

Where Can You Find Psychology Funding?

Students planning to study psychology can also explore bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities that may be available for their studies.

Eligibility requirements, application dates and funding conditions differ between providers. Students should review each funding opportunity carefully and confirm the latest information directly with the relevant funding provider.

Important Information

The availability of psychology qualifications and programmes can change, and not every institution offers the same undergraduate or postgraduate options.

Always consult the official website of the relevant institution for current information about qualifications, admission requirements, applications and programme structures before making decisions about your studies.
