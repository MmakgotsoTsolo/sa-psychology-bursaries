How to Prepare for a Funding Interview

Information about funding interviews and what students may consider when preparing for an interview for educational funding.

What Is a Funding Interview?

A funding interview is a conversation between an applicant and a funding provider or its representatives. It may form part of the selection process for certain bursaries, scholarships, grants or other funding opportunities.

Not every funding opportunity includes an interview. Where an interview is required, the funding provider will generally provide information about the interview process and any relevant requirements.

Review the Funding Opportunity

Before attending an interview, students should review the information provided by the funding provider about the funding opportunity.

This can help students understand the purpose of the funding, its eligibility requirements and any information that may be relevant to the selection process.

Understand Your Application

Students should be familiar with the information they provided in their funding application.

An interview may include questions about academic information, educational goals, achievements, experiences, financial circumstances or other information included in the application.

Be Prepared to Discuss Your Studies

Students may be asked about their current or intended studies and their academic interests.

Applicants should be able to explain what they are studying or intend to study and why their chosen field is relevant to their educational or professional goals.

Consider Your Educational and Career Goals

A funding interview may provide an opportunity for students to discuss their future educational and career plans.

Students can consider how their studies may contribute to their future goals and the knowledge or skills they hope to develop.

Answer Questions Clearly

Students should listen carefully to each question and answer it as clearly and honestly as possible.

There is no need to provide an unnecessarily complicated answer. Applicants can take a moment to consider a question before responding and should focus on addressing what has been asked.

Discuss Your Experiences

Depending on the funding opportunity, students may be asked about their academic achievements, leadership, community involvement, extracurricular activities or other experiences.

Applicants should consider which experiences are relevant to the funding opportunity and be prepared to explain what they learned from them.

Present Yourself Professionally

Students should approach the interview in a respectful and professional manner.

This includes communicating clearly, listening to the interviewers and following any instructions provided by the funding provider regarding the interview format, location or technology.

Prepare for an Online Interview

If the interview takes place online, students should review the instructions provided by the funding provider and ensure that they understand how to access the interview.

Students should check their internet connection, device, microphone and camera in advance where these are required for the interview.

Ask Questions When Appropriate

If the interviewer provides an opportunity for questions, students may ask relevant questions about the funding opportunity or the next stages of the process.

Applicants should avoid asking questions that have already been clearly answered in the information provided by the funding provider.

Important Information

Funding interview processes differ between funding providers. Some funding opportunities may not require an interview, while others may have specific interview requirements or selection processes.

Always follow the information and instructions provided by the relevant funding provider when preparing for and attending a funding interview.

Following the information provided in this article does not guarantee that an application will be successful or that funding will be awarded. Funding decisions are made by the relevant funding provider according to its own eligibility requirements, selection criteria and available funding.
