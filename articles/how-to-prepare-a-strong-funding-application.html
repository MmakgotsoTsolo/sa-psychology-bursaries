How to Prepare a Strong Funding Application

Information on how to prepare a strong funding application by understanding the requirements, organising your information and reviewing your application before submission.

What Is a Funding Application?

A funding application is a formal request for financial support towards education or related study expenses. Depending on the provider, funding may be offered through a bursary, scholarship, grant or another form of student funding.

Each funding provider may have its own eligibility requirements, application process and supporting documents. Applicants should therefore approach each application according to the instructions provided by the relevant funding organisation.

Start by Understanding the Funding Opportunity

Before preparing an application, carefully read the information provided by the funding provider. This can help you understand what the funding is intended to support and whether the opportunity is suitable for your circumstances.

Pay attention to the eligibility requirements, fields of study, study level, application dates, required documents and any other conditions specified by the provider.

Understanding these requirements before starting an application can help you avoid spending time on an opportunity for which you may not qualify.

Check the Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility requirements can differ significantly between funding opportunities. Some providers may consider factors such as academic performance, household circumstances, field of study, study level, citizenship or other criteria.

Review every requirement carefully and consider whether you meet the criteria before submitting an application.

If you are uncertain about a requirement, consult the official funding provider rather than relying on assumptions or information from unofficial sources.

Prepare Your Information

Having the necessary information available before beginning an application can make the process easier and reduce the likelihood of entering incomplete or incorrect information.

Depending on the funding opportunity, you may need information about your personal circumstances, academic history, current studies, intended studies or financial circumstances.

The information required will depend on the funding provider. Always follow the application instructions provided by the organisation offering the funding.

Organise Your Supporting Documents

Funding providers may require applicants to submit supporting documents as part of the application process. Preparing these documents in advance can help you complete the application more efficiently.

Documents may differ between funding opportunities and can include academic records, identification documents, proof of registration or admission, financial information or other supporting evidence.

Applicants should check the specific document requirements of each funding opportunity and ensure that documents are clear, current and in the required format.

For more information about documents that may be required, see our article on what documents you may need when applying for funding

Provide Accurate Information

All information provided in a funding application should be accurate and consistent with the supporting documents submitted.

Applicants should take care when entering personal, academic and financial information and should review their answers before submitting the application.

Providing false or misleading information can affect an application and may have consequences depending on the terms of the funding opportunity.

Prepare Your Motivation

Some funding providers may ask applicants to provide a motivation or personal statement as part of the application. Where this is required, the applicant should follow the instructions provided by the funding organisation.

A motivation can provide an opportunity to explain your circumstances, academic goals, reasons for applying and how the funding may support your studies.

The requirements for a motivation can differ between funding opportunities. Applicants should therefore avoid using the same response without considering the specific opportunity they are applying for.

For further guidance, see our article on how to write a funding motivation letter

Review the Application Before Submitting

Before submitting a funding application, review the entire application carefully. Check that all required sections have been completed and that the information provided is accurate.

Make sure that all required supporting documents have been attached and that they meet the provider's requirements.

It can also be useful to review the application for spelling, incomplete answers, incorrect information and other avoidable errors.

Keep a Record of Your Application

After submitting an application, keep a record of the information submitted and any confirmation received from the funding provider.

Depending on the application system, you may receive a confirmation email, reference number or other indication that the application was submitted successfully.

Keeping these records can make it easier to refer to your application if you need to communicate with the funding provider later.

Important Information

There is no single application process that applies to every funding opportunity. Bursaries, scholarships, grants and other forms of funding may have different requirements and application procedures.

Always follow the instructions provided by the relevant funding provider and confirm the latest information through its official website before submitting an application.

Following the information provided in this article does not guarantee that an application will be successful or that funding will be awarded. Funding decisions are made by the relevant funding provider according to its own eligibility requirements, selection criteria and available funding
