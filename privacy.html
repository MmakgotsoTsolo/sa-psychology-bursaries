Privacy Policy

SA PSYCHOLOGY BURSARIES RESPECTS YOUR PRIVACY AND IS COMMITTED TO PROTECTING THE INFORMATION ENTRUSTED TO US. THIS PRIVACY POLICY EXPLAINS HOW INFORMATION MAY BE HANDLED WHEN YOU VISIT, USE OR CONTACT THIS WEBSITE

Information We Collect

SA Psychology Bursaries does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors simply for browsing or using the website.

You can access and browse the funding information provided on this website without creating an account or submitting personal information to us.

Contact Emails

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you provide to respond to your enquiry and improve our website where appropriate.

Information voluntarily provided to SA Psychology Bursaries will not be sold, rented or shared with third parties. Information received through email will be used only for the purpose for which it was provided, including responding to enquiries, addressing website-related matters, receiving feedback or communicating with you regarding your message, unless disclosure is required by law.

Please do not send personal or sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry. This includes identification numbers, copies of identity documents, banking or financial information, passwords, account credentials, medical information or other sensitive personal information. SA Psychology Bursaries does not require such information for general enquiries or website-related communication.

We encourage users to exercise caution when communicating online and to provide only the information reasonably necessary for us to respond to their enquiry.

External Websites

This website contains links to external websites operated by funding providers and other third parties. Once you leave SA Psychology Bursaries and access an external website, SA Psychology Bursaries is not and will not be held responsible for that websites website's own privacy policy, terms and practices.

Website Analytics and Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services, such as Google Analytics to understand website usage and measure performance. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by the website or third-party services to support functionality, understand how visitors interact with the website and analyse website traffic. You may manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings.

Policy Updates

SA Psychology Bursaries reserves the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to remain informed about how information is handled.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns or enquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled by SA Psychology Bursaries, you may contact us using the email address provided on our Contact page.
