Tips for Submitting a Funding Application

Information to help students review their information, supporting documents and application before submitting a funding application.

Review the Funding Requirements

Before completing an application, carefully review the eligibility requirements and application instructions provided by the funding provider.

Requirements may differ between bursaries, scholarships, grants and other forms of educational funding. Students should make sure that they meet the relevant requirements before submitting an application.

Check the Application Deadline

Take note of the application opening and closing dates provided by the funding provider.

Students should allow enough time to complete the application and gather any required supporting documents before the closing date.

Do not assume that an application will be accepted after the stated deadline. Always follow the funding provider's instructions regarding submission deadlines.

Prepare Your Information in Advance

Having the information required for an application organised in advance can make the application process easier to manage.

Students may need to provide personal, academic, financial or other information depending on the requirements of the funding opportunity.

Review the application carefully and make sure that the information provided is accurate and complete.

Check Your Supporting Documents

Review the list of supporting documents required by the funding provider before submitting your application.

Documents should be clear, legible and submitted in the format requested. Students should also check whether any documents need to be certified or whether additional requirements apply.

Make sure that the documents attached to the application are the correct documents and that they are accessible and readable.

Complete Every Required Section

Before submitting an application, review each section to make sure that all required information has been provided.

Leaving required fields incomplete may affect the processing of an application. Students should follow the instructions provided by the funding provider rather than making assumptions about what information is necessary.

Review Your Application Before Submitting

Take time to read through the completed application before submitting it. Check names, contact details, academic information and other important details for accuracy.

It is also useful to check that the correct supporting documents have been attached and that any required questions or statements have been completed.

Keep a Record of Your Application

After submitting an application, students should keep a record of the application and any relevant confirmation received from the funding provider.

Where possible, keep copies of submitted information and supporting documents for your own records.

If the funding provider provides an application reference number or confirmation of submission, keep this information in a secure place.

Follow the Funding Provider's Instructions

Each funding provider may have its own application process and submission requirements. Students should follow the instructions provided by the relevant funding provider throughout the application process.

If the application instructions are unclear, contact the funding provider directly for clarification before submitting the application.

Important Information

The information provided in this article is general information intended to help students review and prepare their funding applications. Application requirements and processes may differ between funding providers.

Always consult the official website or application instructions of the relevant funding provider for the most current requirements before submitting an application.

Following the information provided in this article does not guarantee that an application will be successful or that funding will be awarded. Funding decisions are made by the relevant funding provider according to its own eligibility requirements, selection criteria and available funding.
