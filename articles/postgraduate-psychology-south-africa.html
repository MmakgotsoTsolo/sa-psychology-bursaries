What Is Postgraduate Psychology in South Africa?

An overview of postgraduate psychology study, the different levels of postgraduate education and what further education and training may be required for professional psychology careers.

What Is Postgraduate Psychology?

Postgraduate Psychology refers to further study in Psychology after completing an appropriate undergraduate qualification. It allows students to develop more advanced knowledge and skills in Psychology and may involve further academic study, research, practical training or professional preparation.

Postgraduate Psychology can involve different levels of study, including Honours, Master's and Doctoral qualifications. The qualification and pathway a student follows depend on their academic background, interests and future career objectives.

The structure and requirements of postgraduate Psychology can differ between institutions and qualifications. Students should review the official programme information of the institution they are considering to understand the available options.

What Is an Honours Degree in Psychology?

An Honours degree in Psychology is a postgraduate qualification that allows students to develop more advanced academic knowledge and research skills in Psychology.

An Honours programme may include advanced Psychology modules, research methods, psychological theory and a research component. The exact structure depends on the institution and qualification.

Admission requirements also differ between institutions. Students may need to have completed an appropriate undergraduate qualification and meet specific academic requirements before they can apply.

Students should consult the official programme information of the institution they are considering for the current admission requirements and programme structure.

What Is a Master's Degree in Psychology?

A Master's degree in Psychology is a further postgraduate qualification that allows students to develop more specialised knowledge and advanced skills within Psychology.

Master's programmes can differ in their structure and purpose. Depending on the qualification, a programme may place greater emphasis on research, academic study, practical training or professional preparation.

Students considering a Master's degree should review the admission requirements, programme structure, areas of study and expected outcomes of the specific qualification before applying.

What Is a Doctoral Degree in Psychology?

A Doctoral degree in Psychology is an advanced postgraduate qualification that generally involves substantial independent research within a specialised area of Psychology.

Doctoral study allows students to develop advanced research knowledge and contribute to the existing body of knowledge within Psychology.

The admission requirements, research requirements and duration of a Doctoral programme can differ between institutions and qualifications. Students should consult the official programme information before applying.

How Long Does Postgraduate Psychology Take?

The length of postgraduate Psychology study depends on the qualification, institution and programme structure.

An Honours qualification may take around one year of full-time study, while Master's and Doctoral qualifications may take longer depending on the programme requirements, research component and whether the student studies full-time or part-time.

Students should check the official programme information of the institution they are considering for the expected duration and requirements of the qualification.

Can You Become a Psychologist With Postgraduate Psychology?

Completing a postgraduate Psychology qualification does not by itself mean that a person is qualified to practise as a registered psychologist in South Africa.

Professional psychology careers involve specific education, training, practical and registration requirements. These requirements can differ depending on the professional psychology category a student intends to pursue.

Students interested in becoming registered psychologists should research the requirements for their intended professional pathway and consult the relevant official organisations before making decisions about their education and training.

What Can You Do With Postgraduate Psychology?

Postgraduate Psychology can provide students with more advanced knowledge and skills that may be relevant to further academic study, research and different employment contexts.

The opportunities available to a graduate depend on the qualification completed, the area of study, additional education and training, and any professional registration requirements that may apply.

Students interested in a particular career should research the education, training and professional requirements associated with that career before selecting a postgraduate pathway.

Where Can You Study Postgraduate Psychology?

Postgraduate Psychology is offered by higher education institutions across South Africa. The availability of Honours, Master's and Doctoral qualifications can differ between institutions.

Students can research institutions that offer postgraduate Psychology and compare the qualifications, areas of study, programme structures and admission requirements available to them.

Students should consult the official website of the institution they are considering to confirm the current availability, requirements and application process for the qualification they wish to pursue.

Where Can You Find Postgraduate Psychology Funding?

Students considering postgraduate Psychology can explore bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities that may support their studies.

Eligibility requirements, application dates and funding conditions differ between funding providers. Students should review the requirements of each funding opportunity and confirm the latest information directly with the relevant funding provider.

Important Information
