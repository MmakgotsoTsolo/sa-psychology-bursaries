Postgraduate Psychology Funding

Explore Honours, Master's and Doctoral funding opportunities available to postgraduate psychology students in South Africa.

The funding opportunities listed below are provided for informational purposes.

Eligibility requirements and application dates are determined by each funding provider and may change. Please visit the provider's official website to confirm eligibility and application dates.

Be sure to check back regularily, as new funding opportunities will be added and existing listings may be updated.
