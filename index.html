Welcome, Future Psychologists!

Welcome to SA PSYCHOLOGY BURSARIES, a website developed in 2026 to help undergraduate and postgraduate psychology students across South Africa easily find funding opportunities in one place.

We understand how stressful and time-consuming it can be to search for psychology bursaries, scholarships and other funding opportunities. Our aim is to make the process easier by bringing useful funding information together on one website.
